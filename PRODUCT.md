# Product

## Register

product

## Users

OMASPRITE serves three audiences in one progressive workspace: children and first-time creators, hobbyist pixel artists, and advanced indie developers. A creator should be able to open the app, paint a small scene, press Play, and understand the next useful capability without studying a manual first.

## Product Purpose

OMASPRITE is an open-source native Linux game editor for building sprite-based 2D and 2.5D games. The Game Editor Beta proves the core creation loop: create a project, paint/edit sprites, arrange a tilemap, preview animation, inspect assets, and play a small scene. Its defining feature is an adaptive creator level that reveals complexity progressively instead of hiding power behind a permanently dense interface.

## Brand Personality

Playful, imaginative, capable. The product should feel like a bright pixel workstation from a friendly game-making future: expressive enough for kids, precise enough for serious creators, and never embarrassed by its tools.

## Anti-references

Avoid a generic SaaS dashboard, a dark intimidating professional IDE, a toy-like children's app that hides real control, and a clone of Aseprite or Picotron. Avoid decorative gradients, unreadable micro-labels, and fake integrations or sample activity presented as user data.

## Design Principles

- Start with a playable result, not a configuration maze.
- Reveal power at the moment it becomes useful.
- Make the canvas the hero and keep tools legible around it.
- Treat pixel art as authored craft: crisp edges, deliberate palettes, clear layers.
- Keep projects local-first, human-readable, and friendly to Git.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA fundamentals for the editor UI: keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus, readable contrast, non-color status cues, reduced-motion support, scalable text, and clear labels. The visual language can be vivid without requiring color vision to understand state or progress.
